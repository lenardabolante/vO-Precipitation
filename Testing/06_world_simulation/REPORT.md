## Bugs found and fixed

1. **Rain on the deserts, none on the rain forests** (Precipitation and Clouds layers).
   Symptom: the heaviest rain of the map lay on the Sahara and Arabia in July and on central Australia and the Atacama in January; the Amazon, the Congo and Indonesia were dry in both months.
   Root cause: `sim/worldsim.py` `_build_grid` pushed the land rain band 70 % farther from the equator than the ITCZ (to 24.6N in July, 18.6S in January), the monsoon term was centred at 20 degrees and 11 wide, and there was no term for year-round equatorial rain over land.
   Change: new constants `ITCZ_LAND_OVERSHOOT = 0.10` (was 0.7 inline), `MONSOON_LAT = 14.0`, `MONSOON_WIDTH = 8.0` (were 20 and 11), `EQUATORIAL_WIDTH`, `EQUATORIAL_CLOUD`, `EQUATORIAL_RAIN` and an `equat` term in `_row_climate` and `_build_grid`. Location dots are untouched (they read the presets).
   Evidence: before/after figures in row 3; pictures `05_`, `06_`, `04_` with `_before_fix` twins. Regression test `test_the_rain_falls_on_the_rain_forests_and_monsoon_lands_not_on_the_deserts` in `sim/tests/test_worldsim.py`: fails on the old constants ("0.79 not less than 0.15 : In Salah in July"), passes now.
   This was asked as an advisory question (Q1) and applied on its default when no answer came.
2. **Sun icon in the polar night.** Root cause: `WorldPainter.java` and `WorldScreen.java` decided day or night from the local clock (06:00-19:00). Now `WorldPainter.nightAt` uses the frame's subsolar point. Evidence: row 10.
3. **`--click` on a dot's own coordinates missed a dot that lies in the sea on the map (McMurdo).** Root cause: `ModeSnapshot.java` called `clickGeo`, which has no dot test. New `WorldScreen.clickPlace` does the dot test first; `ModeSnapshot.java` line 324 now calls it (one call changed in a shared file). Evidence: the run for `31_card_antarctica_december_click_on_dot.png` prints "selected antarctica".
4. **Yellow line down the map when Antarctica is selected** (also Russia and Fiji, whose polygons are cut at 180). Root cause: `WorldPainter.paintRegion` stroked the closed polygon including its artificial edges. `WorldMap.java` now keeps a seam-free `Region.outline` built with the existing `outline()` helper. Evidence: row 22.
5. **A dot under the pointer read as its region on the first frame.** Root cause: `WorldScreen.computeHover` runs before the dots are placed by the painter. New `WorldPainter.placeDots`, called before the hover test. Evidence: `34_hover_dot_tooltip_japan.png` (before: "Japan / click: Japan"; after: "Japan · 16.9 °C, cloudy").
6. **Dot label under the legend box.** `WorldPainter.avoid` (set from `WorldScreen.layout`) is added to the rectangles labels keep clear of. Evidence: `37_card_washington_us_state_click_on_state.png` (Hawaii's label now above the legend).
7. **"-0°" printed** for readings between -0.5 and 0. New `WorldModel.whole`; used for the dot label, the list, the nearest-location line and the chart axis. Evidence: `41_layout_1280x720_overview_temp.png` (Alaska "0°").
8. **"Mean temperat…" cut off** in the overview at 1280 px and narrower. `WorldScreen.overview` now writes "Mean temp." when the long caption does not fit. Evidence: `41_`, `44_`, `50_`.

## Bugs found and not fixed

1. **Rain share against the monthly normals is wrong-signed for Moscow and flat for Munich** (row 17). `sim/worldsim.py` `rain_chance` (about line 830) gives cold months more wet days per millimetre (3 mm per wet day in the cold against 10 mm in the warm), and `_location` adds rain near lows, which are winter-weighted (`_season_weight`). For continental places with a summer maximum this outweighs the monthly total. Not fixed: the existing tests pin this formula (`test_rain_chance_follows_the_monthly_total` asserts that cold light precipitation falls on more days), so changing it is a design decision, and one seed over three years is a small sample.
2. **No sea ice.** `sim/worldsim.py` `_build_grid`, the line `snow[i] = ... * (1.0 if fl >= 0.8 else 1.25 * fl)` (about line 740): snow is scaled by the land fraction, so the Arctic Ocean is open blue water in January under a legend that says "Snow and ice cover" (`11_layer_snow_january.png`). Not fixed: adding sea ice is a new feature and changes the look.
3. **The sandbox opened from a mountain preset shows a much warmer headline temperature than the card.** Kenya card 23.1 C at 12:58 in July, sandbox "29.7 °C at the surface" (`28_` against `57_`); Meghalaya card 24.6 C, sandbox 33.7 C (`29_` against `58_`). Cause: the card reads the station (Nairobi, Cherrapunji, both high up) while the sandbox reports the temperature at the lowest ground of the transect (PROTOCOL.md, `world.surface_temp_c`). Owned by the sandbox-world area (`sim/simulation.py`, `sim/presets.py`); for the orchestrator to route. Oklahoma (24.1 against 27.0, the card is under a storm) and the Amazon (28.7 against 28.3) agree.
4. **Glyphs and tags overlap where presets crowd** (North America at whole-world zoom, worst at 1040x620): the "Tornado outbreak" tag and the L glyphs are drawn before the dot labels and are partly covered (`WorldPainter.java` `paintSystems`, the `tag(...)` calls near lines 914 and 961; `paintDots` places labels without knowing the system glyphs). Seen in `44_`, `50_`. Not fixed: needs a placement rule, which is a design change.
5. **The help list names keys "1 2 3 4 5 6"** but key 7 (Map) works as well (`WorldScreen.java` `helpOverlay`). Text only; left as is.
6. **Riyadh and Tamanrasset still read 0.30-0.35 in July** after fix 1 (edge of the monsoon band on a 5-degree grid), and the grid cell over Cherrapunji reads 0.02 in July while its dot rains 84 % of the time (a single wet mountain face cannot show on this grid).
7. **`--selftest-ui` step 47** ("] slides the right panel away", sandbox HUD, not this area) failed once in my run at 100 % CPU and passes in the baseline log; the world steps 29-44 passed. Looks like a timing-sensitive check.

## Things that look questionable but are design choices

1. The Temperature layer is a sea-level field, the dots are station readings. Under a mountain dot the map colour and the dot disagree: Tibet dot -7 C on a +13 C map in January, Atacama +13 on +32, Colorado -6 on +4, Sahara +45 on +35 in July (the model's own stated Sahara bias).
2. The model runs warm: global mean 16.9-18.6 C against about 14 C for the real Earth; the Southern Ocean at 65S reads +6.5 C in January.
3. The rain band travels farther than the real one: its sea maximum sits at 17.5N in July (real about 8-10N).
4. One sim day is 300 s and a year is 8 days, so the calendar moves 1.5 months per day and the clock 0.08 h per second. A snapshot taken with `--month` and `--hour` glides there for up to 22 s while the clock keeps running; the pictures were timed for this (see Commands).
5. "Storm" is shown for a place near a strong low even in cold weather (Tokyo, 5 C, January).
6. PLAN open question 18 (the look of the flat data map) is unchanged. Honest observations: the Temperature and Precipitation layers are full-spectrum colour fields that cover the land completely at whole-world zoom; the Precipitation layer in particular is a saturated rainbow band (green, yellow, red, magenta). The plain Map and Clouds layers look calmest. Nothing was restyled.
