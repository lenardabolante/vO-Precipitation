## Bugs found and fixed

1. **Puddles and snowpacks only ever grew.** One shower left a 140 px puddle and one snowfall a 160 px white drift for the rest of the world's life (measured: unchanged after 600 s dry and warm). Root cause: `ObjectField.update` raised `w` / `h` and never lowered them. `sim/objects.py`: `_shrink()` lowers the size with the fading `wet` / `snow` back to the generated 20x3 / 20x2; the unrounded size is kept in `size_f` and saved (optional key, old files load). Tests added in `sim/tests/test_objects.py`: `test_puddles_dry_up_and_snowpacks_melt_away_again`, `test_a_shrinking_puddle_survives_save_and_load_frame_for_frame`. After: puddle 140 → 67.9 px at 60 s → 20 px at 120 s; snowpack 160 → 118 → 75.9 → 20 px at 300 s.
2. **White snow heaps and blue puddles on a dry 15 °C street.** Root cause: the viewer drew every puddle / snowpack object at its starting size whether or not it held water or snow. `Renderer.java` `drawPuddle` / `drawSnowpack` (SHARED, one condition each): nothing is drawn while `wet` / `snow` is 0. Before `07_settlement_town_day_before_fix.png` (three white heaps on the road); after `07_settlement_town_day.png`.
3. **A settlement name lay across the bottom of the region card.** `Renderer.java` `drawSettlementLabels` (SHARED, +2 lines): a label under the left panel moves to the right of it. Before / after: `22_preset_tibet_region_card_hud_before_fix.png` / `22_preset_tibet_region_card_hud.png`.
4. **Ruins kept lit windows and bent lamps kept their glow at night.** Root cause: damage cuts pieces out of the building's intact image, lit windows included; a damaged lamp is the intact lamp rotated, glow and light pool with it. `BuildingPainter.java`: `Ctx.on()` puts windows out between 0.2 and 0.65 damage, the street lamp's light fades with damage, a wrecked shop sign is unlit. Before `31_damage_adobe_sahara_night_before_fix.png`, `31_damage_hut_kenya_night_before_fix.png`; after the same names without the suffix. Partly damaged tower blocks keep some lights (`31_damage_church_townhouse_apartment_plain_night.png`).
5. **`--bench` ignored `--cmd` and `--until` with the real simulation.** The help says `--until` runs "then take the picture / start the bench"; the first tornado bench reported "a tornado in the world on 0 of 600 frames". `Bench.java`: the real-sim branch now sends the commands (timed ones included) and waits for the condition. After: 600 of 600.

## Bugs found and not fixed

1. `sim/tests/test_presets.py:652`: Amazon ground snow (row 25). Other area's files (`sim/clouds.py`, `sim/simulation.py`).
2. `sim/objects.py` `flat_spot` (windsock placement, about line 183): 213 of 240 windsocks stand on a building, 5 in a pond. The flattest spots are the levelled settlements. Not fixed because any change alters generation of every existing seed and `test_plain_worlds_are_byte_identical_to_before` pins it; needs a decision.
3. `sim/objects.py`, same cause: the puddle and snowpack spots also land in town streets. After fix 2 they are invisible until it rains or snows there.
4. `sim/terrain.py` / `objects.generate_world`: mountainous relief exceeds the documented cap by up to 5 px (row 2).
5. `Renderer.java` near `drawSettlementLabels`: freezing-level pill and settlement pill overlap (row 28).

## Things that look questionable but are design choices

1. Landmark elevations are real but the freezing level is drawn on the compressed vertical scale, so at 27 °C it sits below the top of a 2,995 m summit in the Amazon. This is what lets row 25 happen.
2. Some settlement names in presets are invented ("Moonstead", "Frostthorpe", "Cinderfield", "Foxhurst") beside real ones.
3. A named town can sit on a high plateau next to its summit marker (Denali, Mount Rainier, Mount Fuji carry a house or a car on top).
4. The custom world has no way to get the biome house and tree variants; they exist only in presets.
5. At "dawn" (05:30) and "dusk" (18:18) the sun is already below the horizon; the picture is red twilight with stars.
6. `--cam` with an unknown car variant falls back to the home view with one stderr line and still writes a picture.
7. The windsock is drawn as large as a shop awning at close zoom.
