## Bugs found and fixed

1. **Sandbox storm clouds rained for about 9 s.** Root cause: the precipitating branch drains 27-32 % of the reservoir per second and leaves the stage at 12 % fill, so a cumulonimbus rained 9.5 s of an 84 s life (Ns 9.4 s of 109 s, As 10.9 s), then faded at about half its stated lifetime. `sim/showcase.py:67` already described this and worked around it for the lesson stage only. Fix: `sim/clouds.py` `update_cloud(..., sustained)` plus constants `RAIN_END_LF`, `SUSTAINED_END_FILL`, `SUSTAINED_RATE`; `sim/simulation.py` passes `self.mode == "sandbox"` at both call sites. Showcase untouched (`test_showcase` 46 OK). After: Cb 119 s of 171 s, Ns 86 s of 151 s, As 52 s of 125 s, Cu 26 s of 67 s. Tests: `test_lifecycle.TestSandboxRainLasts` (3). Side effects, measured: rain total per 900 s went from 931 / 505 / 853 mm to 3,507 / 1,815 / 2,734 mm (seeds 1-3, about x3.5); particles in a default world now mean 878-1,695, peak 2,946 of a 4,496 cap. My first setting (`SUSTAINED_RATE` 0.5) is what produced the 4,465 particles / 24.3 ms the coordinator saw in `test_perf`; at 0.3 `test_perf` reads 2,438 particles, 13.7 ms mean (23.3 ms with a tornado). I did not record `test_perf` before the fix. The three after-fix long runs finished before fix 3 went in. This was Q1, unanswered; the default was applied.
2. **`precip_rate` never reached 0 after the rain.** `round(rate * (1 - 0.8 dt), 3)` is a fixed point at 0.018 (0.037 at speed 0.5, 0.074 at 0.25). Fix: `sim/clouds.py` dissipating branch keeps the value unrounded and snaps to 0 below 0.001. Test: `test_the_rain_rate_falls_to_zero_once_the_rain_stage_is_over`. `test_saveload` 29 OK.
3. **A copied puff could grow 3.0 px a frame in a merge** (test limit is < 3.0; appeared once fix 1 kept an absorbed cumulonimbus fully condensed). Fix: `sim/clouds.py` `begin_merge`, `MERGE_WINDOW_PER_PX`: the growth window scales with the puff radius. `test_supercell` 18 OK, `test_merge` 28 OK. The test was not changed.
4. **Snow lay on the Amazon summit at 24 C** (`test_presets` failure routed to me; ground snow 1.0). Cause: a preset squeezes real relief into the sky, so a 3 km tropical summit pokes above the drawn freezing level; fix 1 made rain long enough to reach it reliably (seed 6 already showed 0.36 with the old engine). Fix: `sim/simulation.py` `_melt_on_warm_ground` (presets only): snow landing where the real temperature, by 6.5 C per real km, is above +1 C counts as rain. Amazon and Philippines 0 snow hits; Antarctica 6,713, Tibet 5,182, Washington 1,856 still snow. `test_presets` 66 OK.
5. **Commands could kill the sim.** `{"cmd":"reseed","seed":1e400}` → OverflowError, process dead; a 400-digit integer in any numeric command did the same; `{"cmd":"wind","delta":1e308}` overflowed the wind grid; two `temp` deltas of 1e308 put `Infinity` on the wire. Fix: `sim/simulation.py` `handle_command`, `_cmd_calendar`, `_cmd_view`; `sim/atmosphere.py` `set_gas`; new limits `WIND_LIMIT` and `TEMP_USER_LIMIT` (±100). Two tests in `test_commands.py` (19 OK). After: the stdin sweep (180 bad values, 12 junk lines) left the process alive with 0 non-JSON lines; the huge-integer and limit cases are covered in-process by the tests.
6. **HUD overlaps** (`viewer/src/precip/Renderer.java`): (a) two cloud labels printed on top of each other at 1280x720; (b) at 1040x581 the storm's label over the left panel title, the freezing label over the Climate panel, the minimap over the cloud-types block; (c) arrow labels over each other and under name plates; (d) the freezing-level pill over a settlement name. Fixes in `drawCloudLabel`, `drawFreezingLabel`, `drawLeftPanel`, `drawArrow` (+ `hitsArrowPill`), `drawSettlementLabels`, `drawWorld`. Before pictures: `Testing/_tmp/sbw/before/`; after: `50`, `52`, `58`. Picture `57` shows arrow labels apart in one scene but is not a before/after of the reported cases.

## Things that look questionable but are design choices

1. `temp` steps the temperature by the whole delta on the next frame, while season / hour / CO2 glide.
2. Gases step at once (PLAN open question 24, confirmed).
3. A merged cloud can be several disconnected groups when both parts are cirrus or altocumulus (20 groups of 55 puffs).
4. Nimbostratus and cumulonimbus are nearly always "mixed" (snow aloft, melting on the way down) below about 25 C at the surface, so most rain starts as snowflakes high in the picture.
5. A bad `reseed` seed restarts the world with seed 0 rather than being ignored.
6. With fix 1 a default world is about 3.5 times wetter and storm clouds live to their stated lifetime; the strength is one constant (`SUSTAINED_RATE`).
7. The low end of the cloud count: one seed dipped to 4 clouds before the fix (target 9-21); the three after-fix runs held 8-21.
